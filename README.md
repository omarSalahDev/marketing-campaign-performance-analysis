<h2>💼 Business Problem</h2>A company has customer-level data covering demographics, spending behavior, purchasing channels, campaign responses, website activity, and customer tenure.

The goal is to answer:

<br><div align="center"><i>"Which customers are more responsive to marketing campaigns, what behavioral patterns are associated with response, and how can these insights support better marketing decisions?"</i>

</div>---

<h2>❓ Business Questions</h2><ul>
<li>What is the overall marketing campaign response rate?</li>
<li>Which customer characteristics are associated with higher response?</li>
<li>How does spending differ between responders and non-responders?</li>
<li>Which purchasing channels show stronger engagement?</li>
<li>Which product categories have the highest spending?</li>
<li>How are recency and customer tenure related to response?</li>
<li>Can customers be segmented based on value and purchasing behavior?</li>
<li>Which customer segment represents the strongest marketing opportunity?</li>
<li>How can the findings be translated into actionable recommendations?</li>
</ul>---
